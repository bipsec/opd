\section{Why it breaks and how to fix it (E3)}\label{sec:mechanism}

E3 uses S1 with two teachers: T4-int and the lowest rung that still trains in E2.

\paragraph{Where the signal is lost.} We bucket response tokens by student entropy and measure
the reward error of the quantized teacher in each bucket (H3).

\placeholderfig{fig:entropy}{Reward error against student-entropy bucket, per rung

\paragraph{Recovery methods} (60 rounds each, all else as in E2), each a drop-in change to
Eq.~\ref{eq:loss-batch} that reduces to it exactly when switched off:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \emph{Reward clipping} \citep{reopold2026}: $r_t \leftarrow \min(\max(r_t,-\tau),\tau)$
        with $\tau\in\{2,5\}$.
  \item \emph{Top-\teacherTopK{} smoothing}: at each sampled prefix, the sampled-token term is
        replaced by the analytic reverse KL over the teacher's top-\teacherTopK{} tokens $K_t$,
        with both distributions renormalized on $K_t$:
        $\sum_{k\in K_t}\tilde p_S(k)\,[\log\tilde p_S(k)-\log\tilde p_T(k)]$.
        With $K_t$ the full vocabulary this is the exact per-position reverse KL.
  \item \emph{Hybrid}: $r_t \leftarrow r_t + \lambda R$, where $R\in\{0,1\}$ is the
        verifier's correctness reward of the whole sequence, added to each of its tokens.
        \todo{$\lambda$ to be fixed before E3}
\end{itemize}
