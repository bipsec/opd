\section{Teacher-signal fidelity (E1)}\label{sec:fidelity}

E1 asks, without any training, how faithfully each quantized teacher reproduces the BF16
teacher's per-token reward on the rollouts the student actually produces.

\paragraph{Protocol.} For each cold-started student we sample a fixed pool of 2{,}000 rollouts
from the \nProbe{} probe prompts and store the exact token ids. Every rung scores the same pool
(top-\teacherTopK{} log-probabilities at every response position), so all differences come from
the teacher.

\paragraph{Metrics} (each against BF16, over all response tokens of the pool):
Pearson correlation of $r_t$; mean absolute reward error $\mathbb{E}|r_t^{(b)}-r_t^{(16)}|$;
KL between the quantized and BF16 teacher over the BF16 top-\teacherTopK{} tokens (renormalized);
top-1 token agreement; the share of tokens whose reward changes sign; and the informativeness
measure of \citet{understandingopd2026} \todo{implement exactly as defined there; ask for the PDF
if ambiguous}. Every metric is also broken down by student-entropy bucket, which feeds H3.
